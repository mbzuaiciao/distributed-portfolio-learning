# Target Journal Recommendation

## Primary target: Quantitative Finance

**Recommendation:** submit first to *Quantitative Finance*.

### Why it fits
The journal's current aims and scope explicitly welcome both theoretical and empirical quantitative finance research and list **portfolio management** and **learning adaptation** among covered areas. The paper combines a stylized analytical framework, a preregistered controlled simulation, and retrospective portfolio evidence, so this scope is a closer match than a journal requiring the mathematical-method contribution itself to be the dominant novelty.

The manuscript should be pitched as:
> an execution-design paper about the interaction between signal decay, transaction costs, noise, and risk aversion in overlapping cohort portfolios.

It should **not** be pitched primarily as:
- an AI/LoRA paper;
- a live trading strategy;
- a proof of real-market global optimality.

### Submission-facing emphasis
1. Lead with the friction-versus-freshness problem.
2. Position Gârleanu and Pedersen (2013) as the closest dynamic-trading benchmark.
3. Explain the distinctive contribution as a discrete overlapping-cohort horizon frontier.
4. Keep the controlled synthetic result central.
5. Use Phases 18/19 as retrospective motivation and reconciliation, not independent confirmation.
6. Preserve all limitations concerning reconstructed returns, no prospective validation, and the finite candidate horizon set.

### Current journal information snapshot
As checked on 5 October 2026, the journal describes itself as an interdisciplinary forum for theoretical and empirical quantitative finance and explicitly includes portfolio management in scope. The journal page reports a 2025 Impact Factor of 1.9, a 23% acceptance rate, and current decision-time statistics. These metrics are volatile and should be rechecked immediately before submission.

## Secondary target: Mathematical Finance

*Mathematical Finance* is a credible but more ambitious second choice. Its scope emphasizes novel mathematical or statistical methods and mathematically rigorous analysis, while welcoming numerical experiments when they support theoretical developments.

The current manuscript has a useful analytical model, but its main contribution is an execution architecture and controlled frontier rather than a deep new mathematical method. Submission there would be more plausible if the analytical section were strengthened substantially—for example, with a more general theorem characterizing horizon choice beyond the stylized variance and turnover approximations.

## Venue decision

**Freeze the first submission target as: Quantitative Finance.**

Do not change the scientific model or run new experiments merely to fit the venue. Venue-specific work should be limited to:
- manuscript length and formatting;
- reference style;
- title page and author metadata;
- figure/table production;
- disclosure/data availability statements;
- cover letter.
