## Accounting limitations carried forward

`build_audited_event_returns` in Phase 9.1 geometrically interpolates day-one to day-30 endpoints into three identical drift weeks, and day-30 to next-earnings endpoints into nine identical drift weeks. This is an endpoint reconstruction, not an observed weekly price tape. Its next-quarter fallback and corporate-action guard are retained and quantified. H=1 is a one-day event return treated as a weekly observation. Horizons count occupied weekly cohort buckets, with gaps and terminal censoring disclosed. Actual intrawEEK execution timestamps and asset price availability cannot be established from these endpoints. These limitations prevent an unqualified confirmation of genuine week-by-week market alpha, even if numerical continuation thresholds pass.

The historical adapter centers probabilities on the full evaluation universe. Phase 18 uses a pretraining-only prevalence constant instead; the common shift leaves simplex Markowitz weights and ranks unchanged. Evaluation labels use the pre-2022 cutoff, correcting Phase 16.1's OOS-quantile diagnostic labels without changing its training or prediction recipe.
